Support

Need help or found a problem? Open a support request. Please include your device, system version and what you were doing. Never post your playlist link or password.

Frequently asked questions

Does Hopwave include channels?

No. Hopwave is a player. You add a playlist from your own provider.

How do I add my provider?

Open Settings → Sources → Add source. Choose Xtream Codes (server, user name, password) or M3U (a playlist link).

The guide is empty for some channels.

Guide data comes from your provider. You can add an external XMLTV guide address to the source.

A stream stops when I watch on another device.

Many providers allow only one connection at a time. Check your plan's connection limit.

Where are adult categories?

They are hidden by default. Turn them on in Settings → Parental controls; a PIN or age confirmation is required.

Subscriptions

Manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings. Purchases are covered by Apple's standard license agreement.
