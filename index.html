Hopwave: IPTV Player & EPG

Watch the playlists you already have on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV. Add your provider's Xtream Codes account or M3U link and Hopwave organises live TV, movies and series, with a programme guide, catch-up and resume.

Features

  • Xtream Codes and M3U / M3U Plus playlists, several sources
  • Plays HLS, MPEG-TS, MKV and AC3/DTS audio
  • XMLTV programme guide and catch-up when your provider supports it
  • Audio and subtitle track selection, preferred languages, resume where you left off
  • Favorites, search, parental PIN, adult categories hidden by default
  • Split view on the foldable iPhone

Important

Hopwave does not include, sell or recommend any content. You need your own playlist from a provider you are licensed to use.
