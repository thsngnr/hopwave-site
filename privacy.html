Privacy Policy

Effective 26 November 2026

Hopwave is a media player. We do not run servers for the app, we do not collect personal data, and we do not use analytics, advertising or tracking.

Data that stays on your device

  • Playlists and accounts. The playlist addresses, provider user names and passwords you add are stored only on your device. Passwords are kept in the system Keychain.
  • Viewing state. Favorites, resume positions, settings and the downloaded channel list and guide are stored only on your device.

Connections the app makes

  • Your IPTV provider. To load your playlist and play streams, the app connects directly to the servers you entered. Your provider's own privacy policy applies to those connections.
  • Programme guides. If you add an XMLTV guide address, the app downloads it from that address.
  • Artwork and information. To show posters and descriptions, the app may send a movie or series title to TVmaze (tvmaze.com) and, only if you add your own TMDB key, to The Movie Database (themoviedb.org). No account or device identifiers are sent.
  • Images. Channel logos and posters are loaded from the addresses in your playlist or the services above.

Purchases

Subscriptions and in-app purchases are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies.

Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports.

Children

Hopwave is not directed at children. Adult categories from providers are hidden by default and can be locked with a PIN.

Changes and contact

We will post any changes on this page. Questions: see the support page.
